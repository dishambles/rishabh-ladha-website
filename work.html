Work

Nine years selling.
Then teaching it.

SquadStack from 2015 to 2024, EPIC World now — and what carried over.

SquadStack

Co-founder & Chief Business Officer · 2015–2024

I co-founded SquadStack (originally SquadRun) in 2015 with no plan to be in sales. We started as a future-of-work platform — combining AI and human intelligence — and evolved into one of India’s largest tech-enabled telesales platforms: 10,000+ telecallers across 67 cities, running enterprise sales motions for clients across BFSI and beyond.

We raised over ₹200Cr ($24M+) across rounds — a Series B led by Bertelsmann India Investments, with Chiratae Ventures, Blume Ventures and others. The founding team was featured in Forbes 30 Under 30 Asia 2018 (Enterprise Technology). I spent nine years there, most of them on the revenue side, building enterprise GTM motions from first call to closed contract.

EPIC World

Head of GTM · Now

Day job: leading GTM at EPIC World, a platform founded on the principles of Elevar Equity, building for India’s entrepreneurial households — the ~250 million households transacting up to $10 trillion a year. With Morningstar Indexes, EPIC launched the EHI Index in 2025: 34 listed companies solving for them, ~$115Bn in combined market cap at launch.

What carried over

The mental models from nine years of building sales teams — the ones I now teach and coach with — are what the Teaching page and my talks are about.
