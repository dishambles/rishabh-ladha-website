05How I work

Four phases, then I’m out of the room.

01

Sit in

I listen to live calls and read the pipeline before changing anything. The problem is usually earlier in the funnel than the team thinks.

02

Coach the seller

Work with the people actually selling — on real deals, in real conversations, not role-play.

03

Codify the motion

What works gets written down as a playbook the team can run without me: qualification, objections, pricing conversations.

04

Hand it over

The playbook belongs to your team, and holds whether I’m in the room or not.

Founder-led sales is a phase, not a strategy.

GTM Dialogues · Delhi · 2025
